## Assurance status

The programme is **internally checked**. It has deterministic replays in clean
temporary directories, an implementation path that avoids SymPy for the replay
challenge, frozen hashes and negative controls. These strengthen internal
assurance but do not constitute independent reproduction.

## Claim ceiling

The package may be described as a completed benchmark-to-experiment programme
for the parent inverse-support theorem candidate, ending in a confirmed
prospective finite degree-32 quartic-lift result. It may not be described as an
all-degree proof, an exhaustive classification of degree-32 phases, a complete
decomposition-lattice computation, a third-party performance victory,
independently reproduced, peer reviewed, formally verified or published.

## Trust base

- parent formal-moment, inverse-support and period arguments;
- exact rational parsing, support reversion and twisted reduction;
- SymPy polynomial composition, decomposition and Gröbner bases;
- the standard-library Fraction replay and shared verification library;
- CPython 3.14.6, SymPy 1.14.0 and mpmath 1.3.0;
- frozen specifications, answer keys, locks, receipts and manifests.

Benchmark timings additionally trust the local operating-system clock and are
excluded from mathematical correctness.
