# Sources

The all-degree proof’s imported classification results, exact theorem
locators, source versions, downloaded-source hashes, group tables, and
application boundaries are recorded in `GLOBAL_COMPLETION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`.
The broader theorem-level comparisons, persistent identifiers, public-index
coverage, and priority boundary are recorded in `CITATION_AUDIT.md` and
`programme/FREE_RESEARCH_ROUTES_AUDIT.md`.

The machine-readable public-route snapshot is
`programme/free_research_routes_snapshot.json`; provider-specific records are
kept separate because zbMATH Open, Crossref, OpenAlex, OpenCitations, and
Semantic Scholar have materially different coverage.

The complete supplied research lineage remains under `source-history/` and
in `MATERIALS_AUDIT.md`. Historical claims are not automatically current
claims. The exact predecessor-to-successor mapping is in
`CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MAP.json`, and the public v0.5 payload is pinned by
`PARENT_v0.5.0_MANIFEST.sha256`.
