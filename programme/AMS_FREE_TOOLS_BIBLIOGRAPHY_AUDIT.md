# AMS free-tools bibliography audit

**Date:** 21 August 2026  
**Tool:** AMS Batch MR Lookup  
**Use boundary:** authorized bibliographic matching; not a full MathSciNet
topic, review-text, or citation-network search

## Result

Fourteen published references received unique MR identifiers:

`MR3149848`, `MR2732247`, `MR2143558`, `MR1692195`, `MR2008728`,
`MR2127946`, `MR2534091`, `MR2443034`, `MR2551466`, `MR1501189`,
`MR1671743`, `MR2454354`, `MR4999549`, and `MR3460044`.

The final identifier is the published 2016 Arnold Mathematical Journal record
for Burda--Khovanskii; the manuscript bibliography was upgraded from the 2012
arXiv-only citation while retaining the preprint URL.  The Combot record is
volume 95, issue 358 (2026), pages 999--1022, MR4999549, with electronic
publication in March 2025.

## Nonmatches

The following title-only queries were returned unchanged:

- Behajaina--Koenig--Neftin, *Monodromy groups of polynomials of composition
  length 2* (2026 preprint);
- Zieve--Müller, *On Ritt's polynomial decomposition theorems* (author
  manuscript/preprint); and
- Kitaoka, *Linear relations among roots of a polynomial* (2018 preprint).

Under the Batch MR Lookup specification, an unchanged row means that no unique
match was identified from the supplied fields.  It is not evidence that the
work is absent from all AMS data.

## Epistemic consequence

The candidate bibliography now has authoritative MR identity checks wherever
the free matcher produced a unique record.  This improves citation integrity
but does not close the missing full topical MathSciNet search or MathSciNet
citation-forward coverage.
