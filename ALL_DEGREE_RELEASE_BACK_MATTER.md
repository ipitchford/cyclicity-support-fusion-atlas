# Limitations

The proof imports named classification results for primitive polynomial
monodromy and finite projective linear groups.  The Lean layer verifies
selected arithmetic, spanning, coefficient, and character consequences; it
does not formalise algebraic-function-field integrality, the direct Dickson
invariant-field argument, Kummer theory, Ritt theory, or the imported
classifications.  The separate CAS implementations check encoded algebraic
consequences, not the source-to-encoding bridge in its entirety.  The two
specialist re-audits are internal and model-assisted, not external peer review.

The literature search used zbMATH Open, complete retrieved OpenAlex citing
sets for named seeds, OpenCitations and Crossref checks for stable DOI seeds,
Semantic Scholar cross-checks, publisher records, and AMS Batch MR Lookup for
bibliography identity.  A dated machine-readable snapshot preserves the
provider-specific counts and their disagreements.  It was not an exhaustive
topical MathSciNet search, all-index citation-forward search, non-English
full-text search, or survey of unpublished work.  No absolute novelty or
priority claim is made.  Positive-characteristic, Laurent-polynomial,
zero-cycle, and arbitrary-amplitude extensions remain outside the theorem.
