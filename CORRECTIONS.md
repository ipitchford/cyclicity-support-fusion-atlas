# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to cyclicity-support-fusion-atlas 0.7.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22049564)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.7.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/cyclicity-support-fusion-atlas/>.

1. Corrected a group name in Section 9, after (9.3). The archived text says the projective monodromy of (9.3) is “the tetrahedral S4”. S4 is the octahedral group, as Section 8 says (the tetrahedral group is A4). The sentence should read “The projective monodromy of (9.3) is the octahedral S4”.

   Only the name is affected. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
