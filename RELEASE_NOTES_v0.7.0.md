# Release notes: 0.7.0-candidate

This additive candidate extends the all-degree fixed-seed Atlas in three
directions.

1. It replaces the fixed seed \(dx\) by every polynomial amplitude
   \(A(x)\,dx\), with an exact primitive-support and reduced-root-span formula.
2. It classifies persistent endpoint moments for all Atlas phases with
   \(q(P)\leq3\), by combining the published polynomial moment theorem with the
   Atlas right-factor list.
3. It gives an exact local inverse criterion for residue moments of arbitrary
   Laurent one-forms, while explicitly declining a false composition-only
   global classification.

The candidate also corrects the interpretation of inherited symmetric Laurent
experiments by recording deck symmetry as a distinct mechanism. It adds a
logarithmic-amplitude control, ordinary/optimised byte parity, semantic
mutation checks, a claim ledger, literature audit, assurance report, and a
visually inspected 12-page paper.

No predecessor paper was removed or superseded. No public publication action
has been taken.
