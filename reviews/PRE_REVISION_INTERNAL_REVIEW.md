## Major findings

### M1. The all-degree atlas is not proved

The v0.3 quartic lift relies on the identity (A(x^m+b)-A(b)=Q_0(x)^m). The supplied verification audit identifies a circular descent. The revised manuscript correctly converts the all-degree classification to Conjecture 8.2 and states the degree-20 theorem. This repair is mandatory and has been made.

### M2. The primitive-group audit needs source-exact expansion

The group eliminations compress several representation-theoretic hypotheses: faithfulness of the projective action, the kernel of the line action, and point-stabiliser characters. This does not affect the support/fusion core, but any future all-degree atlas proof should give a group-by-group lemma with exact citations and character tables.

### M3. Application scripts are not release gates

The zero-cycle program returns success after `REVIEW`, and the Laurent engine prints mismatched expected controls. The manuscript now labels both as exploratory. A field-facing application note must first supply a theorem statement, a proof, a dependency-complete package, and fail-closed tests.

## Devil's advocate counter-argument

The strongest objection is that the headline programme may be mistaken for a complete classification when only its structural reduction is universal. The computational sweep cannot exclude a new degree-32 family, and the representation-theoretic narrative may bias the certificate design toward predicted components. The answer is not more random search. It is the explicit separation now adopted: Theorems 3.2, 5.1, and 6.1 are universal; Theorem 8.1 is finite and certificate-backed; Conjecture 8.2 is open. Any abstract, title, press copy, or application must preserve those quantifiers.
