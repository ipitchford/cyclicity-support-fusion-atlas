## Release decision

**PASS_WITH_NOTES for specialist circulation as an unrefereed candidate.**

The universal inverse-support, cyclic-rank, root-span and Fourier-fusion
claims are supported by the written proofs. The finite affine atlas through
degree twenty is supported by a fail-closed exact-arithmetic sweep of 1,152

non-circular quartic/cubic twisted-factor descent; the first uncovered
quartic lift is degree thirty-two.
