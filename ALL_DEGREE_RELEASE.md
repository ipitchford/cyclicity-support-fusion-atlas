# Abstract

For a complex polynomial phase (P), the cyclic rank generated by the fixed
seed (dx) can be read from the nonzero residue channels of the normalized
inverse series at infinity, equivalently from the reduced constant span of the
generic inverse roots.  Earlier work established this bridge, a Fourier law
for block fusion under polynomial decomposition, and a finite coefficient
atlas.  This successor gives an all-degree classification for cyclic rank at
most three, relative to named results on primitive polynomial monodromy and
finite projective linear groups.  It proves that the only families, up to
affine equivalence and lower-rank intersections, are powers, Dickson
polynomials, quadratic- and cubic-over-power phases, indecomposable quartics,
and the degree-twelve quartic collision.  It also classifies every proper
rank-preserving outer extension: powers extend by powers, Dickson polynomials
extend within one dihedral tower, an odd-centred cubic-over-power phase admits
one aligned square, and the exceptional quartic admits one cubic collision.
The proof replaces an analytic boundedness step by algebraic
invariant-integral descent, supplies the missing full-block stabiliser
interface, repairs a false uniform Kummer-kernel assertion by a
coefficient-stratified theorem, and handles arbitrary mixed Dickson chains by
a direct rational-function invariant-field argument.  Lean and two separate
CAS implementations verify selected local consequences; two separate internal
specialist re-audits pass.  The result remains an unrefereed theorem candidate,
not a complete formalisation, external reproduction, peer-reviewed theorem,
or priority claim.

**Keywords:** polynomial exponential periods; inverse-root support; cyclic
rank; polynomial decomposition; Ritt moves; Dickson polynomials; Kummer
transport.
