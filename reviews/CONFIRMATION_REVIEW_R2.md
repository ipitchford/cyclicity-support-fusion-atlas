## Proof and claim assessment

### Universal core

The revised paper has a coherent theorem chain. The inverse-at-infinity
support is defined in one ambient cyclic group; the formal moment transform
identifies the completed de Rham module with rank-one Kummer channels; the
fixed seed activates exactly the support channels; the discrete Fourier
transform identifies the same channels with the reduced inverse-root span.
The analytic period section is no longer load-bearing for the algebraic rank
identity and independently supplies fixed-sector realisation.

The block-fusion formula is stated for every actual polynomial decomposition
and every block subset. Equality fusion is promoted to an actual polynomial
right factor by the block-system/intermediate-field/Lüroth/one-pole argument.
The complete-decomposition and Ritt-move claims are expressed as projections
of one fixed support, avoiding the false additive law preserved in source
history. No higher-order intersection identity is claimed beyond what the
rank formula proves.

### Finite atlas

Theorem 13.1 now has the quantifiers required for a literal classification:
degree at most twenty, affine equivalence, parameter restrictions,
lower-rank subloci and moduli caveats. Degrees two through four are treated
directly. The finite computation covers every support of size at most three
containing the normalised class one for degrees five through twenty.

The theorem remains computer-assisted. Exact arithmetic and frozen digests
do not remove trust in the inverse-series generator, case enumeration,
source-to-encoding bridge, Python runtime or SymPy Gröbner implementation.
The manuscript and machine claims state that boundary consistently.

### Open classification and applications

Conjecture 13.2 accurately identifies the circular scalar-collapse step and
the first uncovered quartic lift at degree thirty-two. The successful finite
sweep is not used to infer the all-degree assertion. The zero-cycle script's
`REVIEW` outcome and the Laurent control mismatches remain quarantined as
research directions. The generic period-ODE order statement is retained only
with its non-annihilation/generic-functional condition.
