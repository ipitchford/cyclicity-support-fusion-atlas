# Abstract

For a complex polynomial phase \(P\), the cyclic rank generated by the fixed
seed \(dx\) equals the nonzero residue support of the normalised inverse series
at infinity and the reduced constant span of the generic inverse roots. This
paper gives the unified all-degree theory in ranks at most three. Relative to
named classifications of primitive polynomial monodromy and finite projective
linear groups, the coefficient families are exactly powers, Dickson
polynomials, quadratic- and cubic-over-power phases, indecomposable quartics,
and one degree-twelve quartic collision. Every proper rank-preserving extension
is also classified: power and Dickson towers, one aligned square, and one
quartic collision. The proof includes algebraic finite-pole descent, a
full-block stabiliser interface, a coefficient-stratified repair of the
even-exponent Kummer exception, and a direct invariant-field treatment of
arbitrary Dickson chains.

The fixed seed is then replaced by every polynomial amplitude \(A(x)\,dx\).
If \(G'=A\), its cyclic rank is the number of non-trace residue channels in
\(G(\xi(t))\), equivalently the reduced constant span of the values \(G(x_j)\).
This gives the exact rank-zero condition \(G\in\mathbf C[P]\) and a Fourier
annihilation criterion for every specified zero-dimensional cycle. Combining
the Pakovich--Muzychuk polynomial moment theorem with the complete low-rank
right-factor list classifies persistent endpoint moments for every
\(q(P)\leq3\). Finally, for a Laurent phase with zero-pole order \(m\), every
Laurent one-form satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Res}_0L^kA\,dz
=[\tau^{mk-1}]A(\phi(\tau))\phi'(\tau),
\qquad L(\phi(\tau))=\tau^{-m}.
\]

Known indecomposable examples preclude a composition-only all-Laurent
classification, so no finite global Laurent atlas is claimed. Exact replay,
mutation controls, deterministic rendering, and bounded literature audits
support the candidate. It remains unrefereed, not independently reproduced,
fully formalised, or priority-certified.

**Keywords:** polynomial exponential periods; inverse-root support; cyclic
rank; polynomial moments; Laurent moments; polynomial decomposition.

# 中文摘要

對複係數多項式相位 \(P\)，固定種子 \(dx\) 的循環秩等於無窮遠反函數級數的
非零同餘通道數，也等於一般反根的約化常數張成維數。本文統一證明循環秩不
超過三的全次數係數圖譜，分類所有保持秩的外延，並完成有限極點下降、區塊
穩定子介面、偶次 Kummer 例外修正及任意 Dickson 鏈的不變域論證。

若以任意多項式振幅 \(A(x)\,dx\) 取代固定種子，且 \(G'=A\)，新循環秩恰為
\(G(\xi(t))\) 的非跡通道數，從而得到秩零判準與指定零維循環的 Fourier
條件。結合 Pakovich--Muzychuk 多項式矩定理，可逐族分類所有

的全域分類，故本文不宣稱有限的全 Laurent 圖譜。本成果仍是未經同儕審查的
定理候選，並非外部獨立重現、完整形式化或優先權證明。
