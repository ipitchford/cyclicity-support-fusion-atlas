## Claim boundary

- Universal support, cyclic-rank and reduced-root-span equality: written
  theorem candidate.
- Fixed rapid-decay period determinant: written theorem candidate plus
  numerical spot control.
- Fourier ranks, pairwise intersections, equality stabiliser, canonical
  factor and Ritt tables: written theorem candidates plus exact controls.
- Coefficient atlas through degree twenty: finite computer-assisted theorem
  candidate.
- All-degree coefficient atlas: conjecture.
- Generic ODE order: corollary with a non-annihilation boundary.
- Zero-cycle and Laurent programmes: exploratory, non-gated.

No bare word “verified” is used as a substitute for this vector.
