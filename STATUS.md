# Status

Version: **0.6.0-candidate**

Status: **public unrefereed theorem candidate relative to named classification
results**

## Release decision

**GO for public specialist circulation. HOLD for promotion as an externally
established theorem.**

The written successor proof classifies every complex polynomial phase with
fixed seed `dx` and cyclic rank at most three, up to affine equivalence and
lower-rank intersections. It also classifies every proper rank-preserving
outer extension. The proof imports named characteristic-zero primitive
polynomial-monodromy and finite-projective-group classification results.

The producer-side gate passes: sixteen Lean theorem controls, ten independent
exact CAS checks, nineteen repaired Kummer checks, ordinary/optimised parity,
thirteen adversarial receipt mutations per mode, and two separate internal
model-assisted specialist re-audits. The finite degree-20 atlas and the
preregistered degree-32 experiment remain bounded evidence; neither is being
extrapolated into the all-degree result.

## Correction record

Adversarial review disproved the earlier uniform full-Kummer-kernel assertion
on `B != 0`, `A^2 = 4B`, and even exponent. Version 0.6 preserves the
counterexample and replaces the claim with a coefficient-stratified theorem
and exceptional-stratum descent. A separate direct invariant-field argument
closes arbitrary mixed Dickson chains without promoting a finite grid to a
proof.

## Not established

No unaffiliated rerun or reimplementation, complete proof-assistant
formalisation, identified external specialist sign-off, journal peer review,
or settled priority determination has occurred. The imported classification
theorems and source-side Magma calculations are not re-proved here. The
zero-cycle, Laurent-polynomial, positive-characteristic, and
arbitrary-amplitude directions remain outside the theorem.
