# Response to the full review

Thank you for the unusually precise review of v0.4.0-candidate. The revision
accepts the review's central hierarchy: the universal Fourier-support calculus
is the durable result, while the finite atlas must earn its theorem label
through a fail-closed current-byte gate. The prior v0.4 package remains
unchanged; this response accompanies the additive v0.5.0 candidate.

## Principal revisions

1. **The finite atlas now has a release-grade gate.** Every one of the 1,152
   cases is rerun by the default command. An anomaly or missing certificate
   exits nonzero. Ordinary and optimised Python receipts must be byte-identical
   and equal frozen hashes. Each case records its ideal and Gröbner digests,
   classification outcome and reduction witness. Synthetic semantic
   corruptions are required to fail.
2. **The flagship is now self-contained.** The full formal-moment, active
   Kummer, Fourier–Gamma, decomposition-chain, canonical-factor and Ritt
   arguments have been promoted from source history. The eleven diamonds are
   displayed and proved.
3. **The atlas statement is literal.** The theorem now supplies the
   equivalence convention, Dickson normalisation, degree and nonzero
   restrictions, lower-rank loci, direct low-degree proof, realised supports,
   affine moduli and the triangular inverse-coordinate map.
4. **Lineage is byte- and claim-aware.** The prior C1–C10 verification report
   is preserved as historical evidence. The current report uses
   T1–T6/F1/C1/A1, a predecessor bridge, frozen receipt hashes, a complete
   manifest and an external archive-binding receipt.
5. **Application and priority language is narrower.** The abstract no longer
   claims a complete zero-cycle test. The all-degree atlas remains a
   conjecture with its precise circular step and first uncovered degree.
   Recent monodromy work, the exponential Gauss–Manin comparison and the
   Girstmair corrigendum are integrated.

## Residual limitations

The revision does not manufacture assurance that the materials do not
contain. There is no unaffiliated specialist sign-off, proof-assistant
formalisation, fully locked container or second-CAS replay of all 1,152
cases. These remain explicit limitations rather than reasons to weaken the
proved universal core or hide the finite theorem's software trust boundary.

The resulting editorial recommendation is **PASS_WITH_NOTES for specialist
circulation as an unrefereed theorem candidate**. Public publication was not
authorised and has not occurred.
